A defect, not a feature — and the one `SPEC-343` §6 predicted when it closed:

Reported the day after, from ordinary use. Overview said *"Nothing has been checked yet"* and offered
**Check the schematic**. The maintainer followed it, ran the review on the Schematic tab, came back,
and the card had not moved.

## 4. Deviations & Discoveries (Plan Drift)

*   **The daemon was right the whole time, and starting there saved the investigation.** The record
      on disk held `last_reviews: ['schematic']`, and `project_stage.read` computed *"the schematic
      has been checked and the board has not"* from it. Confirming that first meant the search never
      went near the Python, the packs, or the persistence — all of which were working.

*   **Three independent causes, and no single one of them is the bug.** This is why "add
    `last_reviews` to the dependency array" would have been the wrong fix even though it sounds like
    the right one:

    1.  `App.tsx` renders every area at once and hides the inactive ones with **CSS**. Overview never
        unmounts, so nothing re-runs on its own.
    2.  Nothing refetches the project record on navigation.
    3.  The effect's dependencies are the fields Overview changes **itself** — `intent`,
        `kicad_project_path`, `guided_path`. A check run elsewhere moves `last_results` and
        `last_reviews`, which nothing on that page ever sees.

    Fixing only (3) leaves the prop stale from (2). Fixing only (2) still needs (3) to re-run.
    Re-reading on becoming visible sidesteps all three, needs no sibling tab to announce anything,
    and covers a record changed outside the app entirely.

*   **One of my tests could not fail, and the probe is worth recording.** `TEST-018` asserted the
    guard by checking `not.toHaveBeenCalled()` straight after `render`. It passed **before the fix
    existed**. Probing a plain mount showed why: the effect is async, and `projectStage` has been
    called **zero** times at that instant regardless of any guard. It now waits for something that
    does resolve first, so a broken guard has had every chance to fire before the assertion denies it
    did. `CLAUDE.md`'s *"a check that cannot fail is not evidence"*, caught by deliberately looking
    for it rather than by luck.

*   **971 tests passed over a type error.** `active` went into the destructure of `WhereYouAre`
    without being added to its type. Vitest does not typecheck, so the suite was green and `tsc -b`
    was not. Both are needed; neither substitutes.

*   **Verified as a user, which is the whole point of this context existing.** The maintainer walked
    the full progression in the real app: `nothing checked` → **Check the schematic** → `board_only`
    → **Check the board** → `both_checked` → **Generate an enclosure** → `complete`. Four live
    transitions.

    Worth being exact about what the automated evidence was worth here. Every route verified, every
    pack measured against real boards, 968 frontend tests green — and the surface was broken in the
    first minute of somebody using it, in a way none of that could have caught.
