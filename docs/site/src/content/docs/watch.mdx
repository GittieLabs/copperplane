## Share it, or put it in a post

Every cut is on the [Product video release](https://github.com/GittieLabs/copperplane/releases/tag/product-video-2026-10).
Download a file and upload it to the post: feeds play an uploaded video inline, which they will not
do for a link. Each video has two companion files:

- **`-cover.jpg`**: the opening frame, for a platform that asks for a thumbnail;
- **`.srt`**: the captions, for a platform that takes a caption file.
