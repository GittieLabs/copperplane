# CTX-408.8: The Cuts, Made For The LinkedIn Feed

> **Spec Reference:** Links to
> [SPEC-408](../specs/SPEC-408-messaging-for-the-maker-who-is-leveling-up.md)

`CTX-408.7` made the three cuts (15s, 30s, ~60s) and rendered them in 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9. Every one
was reviewed through a phone lens: a 360 px frame from the middle of every caption. The maintainer
then asked for versions for the website and for LinkedIn, and chose **LinkedIn first**.

The cuts already render square. What they do not have is anything made for where they will be
seen:

- **No 4:5.** LinkedIn's mobile feed shows a 4:5 video taller than a square one, so it takes more
  of the screen as someone scrolls past.
- **No cover image.** LinkedIn uses the first frame as the thumbnail unless a cover is set.
  `CTX-408.7` made frame 0 the hook (line set, LED lit, pinned by a test), but nothing exports it
  as a file to upload.
- **No feed-size review.** A phone at 360 px is not LinkedIn's mobile feed, about 390 px wide with
  the post's chrome around it, and it is not the desktop feed, about 555 px wide.

The website version (a 16:9 loop, web-weight encodes, a poster, the docs-site embed) is the next
context, not this one.

## 4. Deviations & Discoveries (Plan Drift)

*   **What LinkedIn requires, and where each claim comes from (checked 2026-10-11).**
    - **Thumbnail:** JPG or PNG, at most 2 MB, matching the video's aspect ratio. From LinkedIn's
      own help page, which covers *video ads*; guides say the same for organic posts.
      `linkedin.sh` fails any cover over 2 MB. They come out at about 90 KB.
    - **From third-party guides only:** MP4 with H.264, 1080p, at least 3 s, 4:5 as the
      feed-friendly ratio, SRT upload for organic posts. The guides disagree on maximum duration
      and file size. All three cuts are far under every figure quoted (61.5 s, 13 MB at most).
    - **Not reached:** LinkedIn's own help page for *organic* video. If an upload is refused,
      start there.
*   **A silent AAC track is added to every upload file. I do not know that it is needed.**
    - Guides describe LinkedIn video as "H.264 with AAC audio", and the renders have no audio
      track at all.
    - Whether LinkedIn rejects a file with no audio track is unverified; the silent track costs
      nothing.
    - Checked that adding it trimmed nothing: every upload file has its source's exact duration
      and frame count (450, 900, 1,845 frames).
*   **The covers are identical across the three lengths.** That is correct, not a copy mistake:
    every cut opens on the same hook frame, which `CTX-408.7`'s test pins at 0 s.
*   **4:5 got its own framing.** The cold open's drawing lies flat in it, because 4:5's media box
    is wider than tall, and fills the width. Reviewed at 390 px (the mobile feed) and 555 px (the
    desktop feed). Scores: hook 8, readability 8, variety 8, composition 8, accuracy 9. Round 5
    in `docs/video/review-log.md`.
*   **Not done:** posting. Phase 4 is the maintainer previewing a post in LinkedIn's composer, on a
    phone.
