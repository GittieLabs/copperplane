# CTX-408.4: Implementation Context: The Docs Site's Logo, First Run, and First Tutorial

> **Spec Reference:** Links to [SPEC-408](../specs/SPEC-408-messaging-for-the-maker-who-is-leveling-up.md)

`CTX-408.3` re-led the docs site's entry path. This adds what a reader actually
needs after arriving: the product's own mark, an honest account of what first
launch asks, and a tutorial built on a real board.

The tutorial is the centre of it. `Copperplane_Blink_LEDs` is an Arduino shield
the maintainer drew, and every figure quoted in the page was measured by running
`kicad-cli` against it rather than invented.

## 1. Feature Definition & Execution Plan

### Phase 1: the screenshots that already existed were unusable
*   [x] All six images in `public/images/` were captured on 2026-08-24 and show
    **"Hardware Agent Studio"** in the window title bar -- the pre-rename product
    name, live on the site since August. `test_readme_claims.py` fails the build
    on that string in prose and cannot read a PNG.
*   [x] They also predate `CTX-338.1`, so every button in them is the old
    near-white accent rather than Copperplane green.
*   [x] Three were replaced from the new walkthrough. Three were **deleted**: a
    missing image beats one naming a product that does not exist, so
    `guides/design-guidance.md`'s reference was removed rather than left pointing
    at a stale picture.

### Phase 2: 40 new captures, catalogued and redacted
*   [x] The path `/Users/<name>/...` appeared in **17 of 40**. `scripts/redact_
    screenshots.py` OCRs each capture, blurs every match, reads the result back,
    and fails if the string survived.
*   [x] Catalogued by a workflow -- ten agents, four images each, about seventy
    seconds -- producing screen, theme, what it shows, and any reason not to
    publish it. Three were rejected on that basis.
*   [x] Dark theme chosen for the whole site. Light captures exist and are
    deliberately unused: a page whose images alternate theme reads as careless.

### Phase 3: the mark, and the site's own colours
*   [x] Starlight `logo` wired to the generated mark, light and dark variants.
    This is the one place a theme pair earns its keep, since Starlight swaps
    them itself.
*   [x] The site's accent was a soldermask green **picked by eye** (`#1c6a5b`,
    teal-leaning) while `brand/README.md` has a soldermask green **by name**
    (`#0B5C34`). Same failure as the app's emerald: a colour that is almost the
    product's colour. Now on the brand palette, roles taken from what the kit
    says each green is for.

### Phase 4: first run, rewritten for the app that exists
*   [x] The page began at "Open Settings" and walked seven manual steps. It
    predates `SPEC-336`'s onboarding entirely -- it was telling people to do by
    hand what the app now offers to guide them through.
*   [x] Rewritten around the three real exits from the welcome screen, with the
    step names and button labels read from `GuidedSetup.tsx` rather than
    described from screenshots.

### Phase 5: the tutorial
*   [x] Five real board errors, the four ERC violations that appear when the two
    `PWR_FLAG` symbols are removed, and the enclosure floor of 14.1mm -- every
    one confirmed by running the real tools against the real project.
*   [x] Its closing section is the defect neither checker reports: a two-pin
    `Device:LED` symbol on a four-pin `LED_D5.0mm-4_RGB` footprint.

## 4. Plan Drift & Architectural Changes

**1. Writing the tutorial changed the shot list, which is why it was written
first.** The maintainer asked whether to shoot before or after. Three concrete
changes came out of writing: `schematic-check.png` turned out to be the tab view
so the ERC-results image needed its own name; `board-check.png` is genuinely a
different image from `board-check-explained.png` and the tutorial uses both;
`guided-done` and `project-linked` were dropped as pictures no page needs. Twelve
slots became eleven, three of them differently framed. Shooting first would have
meant shooting twice.

**2. The guard caught its author, twice.** `test_docs_images.py` was written to
stop pages pointing at deleted images. It then failed on the rewritten
`first-run.md`, which referenced three screenshots that do not exist yet, and
reported `component-search.png` as orphaned after the rewrite dropped the section
that used it. Both were real mistakes made minutes after writing the check.

**3. I destroyed the maintainer's build.** Seeing `.build-venv` half-deleted, I
diagnosed mystery corruption and recreated it -- twice -- while their rebuild was
tearing it down and rebuilding it. `[Errno 66] Directory not empty: 'python3.11'`
was our two processes racing. `CLAUDE.md` already forbids running the test suite
during a freeze; this was the same rule broken from the other side, against a
freeze someone else owned. The lesson is narrower than the existing rule: when
the maintainer says they are rebuilding, the daemon environment is theirs until
they say otherwise.

**4. Not verified: eleven of the seventeen images the docs want.** Three pages
carry `<!-- screenshot pending -->` markers instead of references, so the guard
stays green and the gap stays visible. The pages read correctly without them, but
`first-run.md` in particular is thinner than it should be until `welcome.png`,
`guided-provider.png` and `guided-tools.png` exist.

**5. The tutorial has not been walked by a person.** Its numbers are real and its
claims were checked against the tools, but nobody has followed it start to finish
in the app. That is the next thing worth doing, and doing it while capturing the
remaining screenshots gets both for one pass.
